The spacefaring maltese pup

A little shop with a story

In 2024, we started as a small gift shop tucked behind an art gallery. Today, Mr. Write is a café filled with second-hand books, coffee, proper tea, and sandwiches worth the wait.

Our story is guided by our mascot: a Maltese poodle who journeyed into space to find "Mr. Right." But after orbiting the stars, they noticed something: the cosmos is very big and very cold, and it notably lacks a good cup of tea.

They realised that "Mr. Right" isn't always a soulmate waiting to be found; it is the warmth of a room, the weight of a book, and the feeling of finally being home.

They traded the cosmos for this colourful, dimly lit corner that feels like the living room of a childhood home. We've kept it that way: a slightly random and very intentional place for community.

Join us and sit quietly with a book/laptop or loudly with friends! We hope you leave with that fuzzy feeling of having arrived exactly where you belong.
